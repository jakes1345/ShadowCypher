How it works

Install once.
Own it forever.

Download the APK

Sideload the APK from GitHub releases. No Play Store needed. No account. Enable "Install from unknown sources" in Settings → Apps.

Download a model

Open Shadow and tap Download. Pick Gemma-4 (recommended) or any GGUF model. Downloads direct from Hugging Face — no PC required.

Set as default assistant

Settings → Apps → Default apps → Digital assistant → Shadow. Now long-press power or use your gesture to summon it anywhere.

Connect to Guardian (optional)

Log in with your ShadowCypher account and Shadow ships security events straight to your Guardian dashboard in real time.

ShadowCypher integration

Shadow watches your phone.
Guardian watches your network.

Connect Shadow to your ShadowCypher account and get a unified view of every security event across every device on your network — from one dashboard.

What gets sent to your dashboard

Shadow ships events to Guardian only if you opt in with your API key. Everything is authenticated. Nothing is shared.
