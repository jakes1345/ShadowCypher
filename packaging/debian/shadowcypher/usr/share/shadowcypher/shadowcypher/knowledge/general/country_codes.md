## IP Geolocation Notes

IP addresses are assigned to country blocks by RIRs (Regional Internet Registries):
- ARIN: North America
- RIPE NCC: Europe, Middle East, Central Asia
- APNIC: Asia-Pacific
- LACNIC: Latin America & Caribbean
- AFRINIC: Africa

IP geolocation is approximate — VPNs, Tor, and corporate networks may show different locations.
