FRESHOODZ DNA

What made their hoodies feel premium and collectible:

  • 400–500gsm heavyweight French terry
  • Enzyme-washed for lived-in softness out of the box
  • Full-scene back graphics — not just a logo
  • Nature + street culture illustration style
  • Bold silhouettes readable at 20 feet
  • Garment-dyed colorways (not dyed-fabric cuts)
  • Flatlock seam construction (no raw-edge rub)
  • Woven brand label, no printed tag

SC ADAPTATION

How ShadowCypher translates that into our world:

  • 450gsm French terry blank — Royal Apparel or Baran Tekstil (Turkish)
  • Enzyme wash / stone wash — same soft-from-day-1 feel
  • Full-scene back graphics — ghost in a world, not floating on black
  • Cyber + streetwear illustration — SC characters in environments
  • Neon glow print method (discharge base + water-based ink on top)
  • Garment-dyed black with cyan/purple overdye options
  • Flatlock seaming, woven SC ghost label at hem
  • Drop shoulder, boxy cut — same silhouette as Freshoodz

RECOMMENDED SUPPLIERS

TURKISH MANUFACTURER · BEST QUALITY

BARAN TEKSTİL / BLANK MANUFACTURER (TR)

Turkish mills produce the closest quality to what Freshoodz used. 450gsm French terry, enzyme wash standard, flatlock construction. MOQ ~100 units. Contact via Alibaba verified or direct at trade shows (Istanbul apparel fairs). 8–12 week lead time. ~$18–24/unit at 100pc.

US DOMESTIC · FASTER LEAD TIME

ROYAL APPAREL (California)

Made in USA, 400gsm+ French terry available, shorter lead times. Higher unit cost (~$28–35) but no import complexity. Good for first runs. royalapparel.net
