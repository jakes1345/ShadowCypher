## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Shadow replaces Bixby, Siri, and Google Assistant with something smarter — a voice AI that knows your network.

Set Shadow as your default assistant app and invoke it with your home button, a squeeze, or just say "Hey Shadow" to get instant, spoken answers to anything.

**What Shadow can do:**

Security (with ShadowCypher account):
• "Scan my network" — triggers a live scan of your devices
• "Any active incidents?" — spoken summary of security events
• "What devices are on my network?" — lists connected hosts
• "Look up CVE-2024-12345" — pulls NVD vulnerability details
• "Check 8.8.8.8" — IP reputation check
• "Have I been breached?" — checks your email against breach databases
• "DNS lookup for example.com" — live DNS resolution

Everyday (works without account):
• Set alarms and timers by voice
• Call and text contacts
• Get weather for any city
• Convert currencies
• Open apps
• Navigate to addresses
• Web search with AI-synthesized answers

**4-layer intelligence:**
Shadow routes your query through the smartest path — OS actions first, trained intent classifier second, local knowledge base third, cloud AI last. Most answers come from on-device in under 200ms. Cloud AI only fires when nothing else matches.

**Offline STT:**
Uses Vosk for on-device speech recognition — no audio ever leaves your phone for transcription.

**Wake word:**
Enable "Hey Shadow" in settings to invoke the assistant hands-free. Runs as a foreground service with ~1% battery impact.

**Privacy first:**
- Speech-to-text runs on-device (Vosk, ~40MB model)
- Wake word detection runs on-device (ONNX, no cloud)
- No ads, no tracking
- Open source: github.com/jakes1345/ShadowCypher
