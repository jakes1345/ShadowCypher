## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Guardian lets you see exactly what's on your home or office network — every device, every connection, every alert — from your phone.

You install the free Guardian agent on a computer you own (a home server, Raspberry Pi, or any Linux machine on your network). The agent scans your own network and sends the results to your Guardian app. That's it.

**What Guardian shows you:**

• Every device connected to your network — name, IP address, manufacturer
• New device alerts — know the moment an unknown device joins your Wi-Fi
• Security incidents ranked by priority so you know what to look at first
• CVE vulnerability alerts matched to the software running on your devices
• Live agent status so you know your monitoring is always running
• Remote scan — trigger a fresh network scan from your phone at any time

**Built for people who take their home network seriously:**

Whether you're a developer, IT professional, or just someone who wants to know what's on their network, Guardian gives you a clear picture without needing to be a security expert.

**How to get started:**

1. Create a free account at shadowcypher.site
2. Install the Guardian agent on any Linux computer you own (one command)
3. Enter your API key in Guardian Settings
4. Guardian connects and starts showing your network immediately

**Your data stays yours:**

• No third-party analytics or advertising
• No selling or sharing your data
• Your network scan data is stored in your own account — you can delete it anytime
• API key is stored only on your device
• Open source: github.com/jakes1345/ShadowCypher

**Why each permission is needed:**

• Internet — to connect to your ShadowCypher account and display your network data
• Network state — to show you whether your phone is connected before making requests
• Notifications — to alert you when new devices join your network or incidents are detected
• Receive boot — to restart background monitoring automatically after your phone restarts
• Vibrate — for notification alerts

**Requirements:**
• Android 7.0 or higher
• A free ShadowCypher account (shadowcypher.site)
• The Guardian agent installed on a Linux computer on your network

Guardian only monitors networks and devices that you own and control. It is not designed for, and cannot be used for, monitoring networks you do not own.
