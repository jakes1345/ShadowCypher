## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Shadow AI is a private voice assistant that runs entirely on your phone — no cloud, no always-on microphone, no sending your voice to a server.

Everything you say is processed on your device using an offline speech recognition model. Nothing is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted. The microphone only activates when you are actively speaking to the assistant.

**What Shadow AI can do:**

• Answer general questions using offline knowledge and web lookups
• Check your home network — connected devices, security alerts, incidents
• Set alarms and timers by voice
• Open apps on your phone
• Works without internet for most commands

**How voice recognition works:**

Shadow AI uses Vosk, an open-source offline speech recognition engine. The speech model (~40 MB) downloads once on first launch and runs entirely on your device after that. Your voice never leaves your phone.

**Optional wake word:**

You can enable "Hey Shadow" to activate the assistant hands-free. Wake word detection also runs on-device with no cloud connection required.

**Optional: Connect to Guardian:**

If you have the Guardian app and agent set up, Shadow AI can answer questions about your home network — devices, incidents, and alerts — by voice.

**Why each permission is needed:**

• Microphone — to hear your voice commands. Only active while you are speaking. Never recorded or stored.
• Internet — to fetch web answers for general questions (like weather or Wikipedia lookups) when you ask for them
• Notifications — for timer and alarm alerts
• Receive boot — to restore your wake word preference after a phone restart

**Your privacy:**

• Voice is processed 100% on-device — never streamed, never stored
• No third-party analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting
• No data is collected about how you use the app
• API key (if used for Guardian) is stored only on your device

**Requirements:**
• Android 7.0 or higher
• Microphone permission (required for voice input)
• ~50 MB free storage for the offline speech model
• ShadowCypher account + Guardian app (optional — for network commands only)
